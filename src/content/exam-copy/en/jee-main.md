JEE Main keeps uploads short: a photo, a signature and a Class 10 PDF for everyone, plus a disability certificate and an ID proof only for some candidates. Like every NTA form, it states file sizes in KB and gives no pixel dimensions.

### What makes JEE Main different

- **Two sessions, one set of files.** Session 1 opens in the autumn and session 2 early in the year. If you applied for session 1 you log in with the same application number later, so keep your files.
- **The signature can be larger than in CUET or UGC NET.** JEE allows 10–100 KB, while those forms stop at 50 KB. Do not copy a JEE signature into them without fitting it again.
- **Class 10 proof is mandatory.** It goes up as one PDF of 50–300 KB.

### Prepare in this order

1. Photograph yourself in colour against a white wall. Your face should fill about 80% of the frame with both ears visible and no mask. Auto-fit keeps a 3.5×4.5 cm shape inside 10–200 KB.
2. Sign on white paper in black ink, crop tight and fit it to 10–100 KB.
3. Scan or photograph the Class 10 certificate or marksheet. Add all pages here and save one PDF between 50 and 300 KB.
4. If you will not verify identity through Aadhaar or DigiLocker, photograph your ID card and fit it to a 10–200 KB JPG.
5. PwD candidates: make the disability or UDID certificate a 50–300 KB PDF.

### Live photo

The form captures a live photo with your webcam or, through a QR code, your phone. It is compared with Aadhaar, so wear what you wear in your recent photo.

### Timing

The session 1 form for JEE Main 2026 ran from 31 October to 27 November 2025. The next session 1 window is likely around November.

### If the portal still rejects it

Read the error: a type error usually means a HEIC or PNG file, and a size error means the file is outside its KB range. Run the same file through the exam preset again rather than a generic compressor.
