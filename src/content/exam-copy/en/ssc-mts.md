SSC MTS (Multi-Tasking Staff) and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) are filled through a single form. The 2025 cycle is the latest we have values for, and the form usually opens around **June–July**. The photo is captured live; the signature is the upload you prepare.

### What you need

- One signature file: JPG, **10–20 KB**, about 6.0×2.0 cm (about 472×157 px at 200 DPI), black ink on white paper.
- A webcam or the mySSC app for the live photo.

Our values are checked against the official MTS 2025 notice on ssc.gov.in (para 10.6). SSC had not published the 2026 notice when we last checked.

### Signing in Hindi or English

You can sign in any script. What matters is that it is your usual signature, it is clear, and it is not tiny. Use the same signature on the attendance sheet at the exam.

### If you use a cyber cafe

Many MTS applicants apply at a cafe. You can still make the signature yourself:

1. Sign on white paper at home with a black pen.
2. Make the file here on your phone and download `sign.jpg`.
3. Send it to yourself or carry it on a pen drive.
4. At the cafe, sit in front of the webcam yourself for the live photo. It cannot be a photo of a printed photo.

### Live photo in a small room

A dim room is the most common reason for retakes. Some fixes:

- Daytime, facing a window.
- A lamp placed behind the screen, pointing at your face.
- No bright window behind you; it turns your face dark.
- Plain wall behind you, no posters.
- No cap, mask or spectacles.

### Why signatures get rejected

SSC states that blurred and miniature signatures are rejected summarily. "Miniature" means a small signature with lots of empty white space around it. A tight crop and Clean signature solve it.

### When the upload fails

Make sure the file is JPG and between 10 and 20 KB. If the size is below 10 KB, run Auto-fit again; it raises quality and, if needed, adds invisible padding to reach the minimum without changing how the signature looks.
