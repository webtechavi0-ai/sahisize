IBPS Clerk (Customer Service Associate) runs on the same portal as IBPS PO, with the same four image uploads. The form usually opens around **August**. Our values come from a reputable secondary source and match the IBPS PO 2026 notice; check the clerk notice when it is out.

### Your checklist

- [ ] Photo, 200×230 px, 20–50 KB
- [ ] Signature, 140×60 px, 10–20 KB
- [ ] Left thumb impression, 240×240 px, 20–50 KB
- [ ] Handwritten declaration, 800×400 px, 50–100 KB

All four are JPG files scanned at 200 DPI. Unlike PO, our sources do not list a 10th certificate upload for clerk.

### Doing it from a phone

Many clerk applicants fill the form on a phone. That works well:

1. Open this page on the phone and tap the kit button.
2. Take the photo with the back camera, held at eye level, while someone else presses the shutter. Selfie cameras distort the face at close range.
3. Photograph the paper with your signature, thumb and declaration flat on a table, from straight above.
4. Crop each item. The crop box is locked to the right shape, so you cannot accidentally make a tall signature or a wide thumb.
5. Download the ZIP, unzip it in your Files app, and pick each file from there in the IBPS upload box.

### The declaration on the IBPS portal

Write the IBPS text shown on this page in English, in running handwriting, with black ink. It has no Date of Birth line. Capital letters are rejected for both the signature and the declaration.

### Common clerk-form trouble

Files under the minimum are as much a problem as files over the maximum. A 15 KB photo fails just like a 70 KB one. If an upload times out on mobile data, your files are still on the device; try again on Wi-Fi rather than making them again.
