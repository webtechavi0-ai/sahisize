HTET asks for three images: a photo, a signature and a thumb impression, all in JPG. The bulletin adds two details that are easy to miss: a white background for both photo and signature, and "at least 60% visibility".

### What "60% visibility" means in practice

Your face should take up at least 60% of the photo. A passport photo cropped loosely, with lots of wall and shirt around you, can fall short. Crop from just above the hair to just below the chin and shoulders; SahiSize locks the crop to the 3.5×4.5 cm shape the bulletin specifies (about 276×354 px at 200 DPI).

### File limits at a glance

- Photo: 3.5×4.5 cm, 20–50 KB, white background.
- Signature: 10–50 KB.
- Thumb impression: 10–50 KB.

### Preparing the thumb impression

1. Use a stamp pad with fresh but not heavy ink.
2. Press the thumb flat on white paper once. Do not roll it.
3. Photograph it from straight above in daylight.
4. Choose HTET → Thumb impression here. Clean thumb turns the paper white and darkens the ridges.

The bulletin does not say which thumb. You also put a thumb impression on the OMR sheet at the centre, so use the same thumb in both places.

### One registration, several levels

You can apply for one or more levels through a single registration, so these three files are uploaded once.

### Corrections

The board opens a short window to correct photo, signature and thumb impression. Outside that window no changes are accepted, so check the files before final submission.

### Timing

These sizes are from the HTET 2025 bulletin. Expect the next form around June–July; we will update this page when the new bulletin is out.

### If the portal still rejects it

A photo error usually means the photo is over 50 KB or not in the 3.5×4.5 shape. A thumb error usually means a smudged print; retake with less ink.
