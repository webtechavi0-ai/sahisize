The MPPSC State Service form has two details that trip people up: the photo must carry your **name and the date**, and the KB limit printed in the advertisement is not the one the portal enforces.

### The KB mismatch, explained

The advertisement allows a photo of 25–200 KB. The online portal has been reported to accept only 20–100 KB. A file that obeys one rule can break the other: 150 KB is fine for the advertisement but fails on the portal, and 22 KB passes the portal but is below the advertisement's minimum. The only range that satisfies both is **25–100 KB**, and that is what SahiSize targets.

### The name and date strip

MPPSC wants your name and the date the photo was taken shown on the photo. SahiSize prints them in black on a white strip under your face. The strip sits inside the final 350×450 pixels; it does not make the file taller. Because the strip uses the bottom of the frame, crop with a little extra room above your head so your face is not squeezed.

### All three files in one go

1. Photo: recent, colour, light background. Crop, type your name and the photo date, then Auto-fit to 350×450 px within 25–100 KB.
2. Signature: black ink on white paper. It is fitted to 350×150 px, 10–100 KB.
3. Address proof: photograph the document flat and fit it to 20–200 KB. Guides list it as an image; check whether your form asks for JPG or PDF before you upload.
4. Download the ZIP and keep it; you can reuse it for the mains form if the specs stay the same.

### Timing

The State Service form has usually opened around January–February.

### If the portal still rejects it

First check the photo is under 100 KB, not just under 200 KB. Then check the pixel size: the photo must be exactly 350×450 and the signature 350×150. These values come from a secondary guide, so if the form states something else, the form wins.
