SSC GD Constable is a little different from other SSC exams. The photo is still captured live, but the signature instructions in the GD notice add a resolution: **6.0 cm × 2.0 cm at 300 DPI**. The 2026 cycle took applications from 01.12.2025 to 31.12.2025, so expect the next form around December.

### What 300 DPI means for you

Most SSC exams work out to about 472×157 px at 200 DPI. At 300 DPI the same 6.0×2.0 cm becomes about **709×236 px**. The file size limit does not change: **10–20 KB**, JPG. SahiSize makes the 709×236 px version for GD and writes 300 DPI into the file.

A larger image with the same KB limit means the background must be clean. Grey paper or a shadow adds weight to the file. Clean signature removes both.

### Signature checklist

- Black ink on white paper.
- **Horizontal**, not slanted. The GD notice asks for a horizontally aligned signature.
- Large enough to fill the box. The notice names "miniature" signatures as the main reason for rejection.
- Your normal signature, the same one you will use at the exam and the PET.

### Thumb impression

The official notice asks only for the signature. Some guides mention a 10–30 KB thumb upload. If your form shows that field, make it here; if not, skip it.

### The live photo

Use good light and a plain background, keep the camera at eye level, and look straight ahead with your whole face in the frame. No cap, mask or spectacles. The notice includes sample photos of acceptable and unacceptable captures; take another shot if yours looks dark or cut off.

### If the signature keeps failing

Check, in order: the file is JPG; it is between 10 and 20 KB; the signature is straight and fills most of the image. Rotate it in the crop screen if it slopes, then run Auto-fit again from the original picture.
