UPSC's Common Application Form has one requirement no other exam shares: you sign three times, one below the other, and upload all three in a single image. That image must be roughly square, 350–500 pixels on each side, and 20–100 KB.

### How to make the three-signature image

1. Draw a square of about 6×6 cm on plain white paper.
2. Inside it, sign three times with a black pen, one signature below the other. Keep them the same size and style.
3. Photograph the square from straight above in daylight.
4. Choose UPSC CSE here. The crop locks to a square; place the corners on your drawn box. SahiSize outputs up to 500×500 px and fits it to 20–100 KB.

One signature, or three signatures side by side, is not what the notice asks for.

### The photo

- Recent, in colour, white background.
- Your face fills about 75% of the frame and both ears are visible.
- Nothing written on it: no name, date or signature.
- File: JPG, 20–200 KB. UPSC gives no pixel size, so SahiSize keeps a 3.5×4.5 cm shape.

### OTR: do it once, carefully

UPSC uses a One-Time Registration profile shared by its exams. The photo and photo ID you upload there carry over, and each application then captures a live photo that is compared with them. If you have grown a beard or changed glasses since you registered, update the OTR photo before you apply.

### Photo ID

Upload the ID whose number you enter in the form, 20–200 KB. OTR guides describe it as a PDF; check the field on the form. Carry the same ID to the exam.

### Timing

The CSE notification usually comes out around January–February, with prelims later in the year. The same Common Application Form also covers the Forest Service prelims.

### If the portal still rejects it

If the signature image fails on dimensions, it is probably not square enough or is under 350 px on a side; crop again from the original photo. If the photo fails, check it is at least 20 KB; small forwarded images are often below that.
