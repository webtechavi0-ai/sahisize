RRB Group D (Level 1, CEN 09/2025) is the largest railway recruitment, and many applicants fill the form on a phone. The form usually opens around **January–February**. You do not upload a photo; you upload a signature, and an SC/ST certificate only if you want a free travel pass.

### Two uploads at most

| File | Format | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signature | JPG, at least 140×60 px | 30–49 KB |
| SC/ST certificate (optional) | PDF | under 400 KB |

### A phone-only walkthrough

1. **Sign** on white paper with a black pen, in running handwriting. Leave space around it.
2. **Photograph** the paper flat, near a window, from straight above.
3. **Crop** in SahiSize. The box is shaped like the 35×20 mm area RRB describes.
4. **Auto-fit**. It enlarges the image and raises quality so the file lands between 30 and 49 KB, the RRB range.
5. **Certificate**: photograph each page, add them here, and make one PDF under 400 KB.
6. **Live photo**: stand against a plain white wall, wear a dark plain shirt, hold the phone at eye level, eyes open, no glasses or cap.

### Why 30 KB?

RRB sets a minimum of 30 KB so that signatures are not tiny or low quality. Do not try to make the file smaller; that is the most common mistake on this form.

### Your signature follows you

The signature you upload is compared at the CBT, document verification, medical examination and appointment. Use the signature you will actually sign with for years, not a special neat version.

### Qualification does not change uploads

Whether you passed 10th, ITI or more, the uploads are the same.

### If the form refuses the signature

Check that the Pass check card shows at least 140×60 px and 30–49 KB, and that the ink is black. If your signature was in blue or in capital letters, sign again; no amount of resizing fixes those.
