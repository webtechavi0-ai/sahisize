For SSC CGL you do **not** upload a photo. The form captures a live photo with your webcam or the mySSC app. The only file most candidates upload is the **signature**. The 2026 window was 21.05.2026 to 22.06.2026, so the next one is likely around May–June.

### The signature, exactly

- JPG/JPEG, **10–20 KB**.
- About **6.0 cm wide × 2.0 cm high**. At 200 DPI that is about 472×157 px, which is what SahiSize makes.
- Black ink on white paper, clear, not blurred, not tiny.

SSC's own wording is that applications with "blurred/miniature signatures" are rejected summarily. A miniature signature is a small squiggle in the middle of a large white image. The fix is a tight crop so the ink fills the box.

### Five minutes to a clean signature

1. Sign on plain white paper with a black gel or ink pen, a bit larger than usual.
2. Photograph it flat in daylight. Keep the phone parallel to the paper.
3. In SahiSize, crop close to the ink. The crop box is locked to the 3:1 shape.
4. Leave Clean signature on. It turns the paper pure white and darkens the ink.
5. Auto-fit lands the file between 10 and 20 KB. A plain signature often saves below 10 KB on its own; Auto-fit fixes that too.

### Getting the live photo right

Find good light and a plain background, keep the camera at eye level, sit straight in front of it, keep your whole face inside the frame, and remove cap, mask and spectacles. Never point the camera at an old printed photo; SSC rejects such forms.

### 6.0 cm or 4.0 cm?

The main instructions say about 6.0×2.0 cm. One annexure of the notice says about 4.0×2.0 cm. Both say "about" and the KB range is identical, so the 6.0×2.0 cm file is the safe choice.

### If it still fails

If the upload is refused, check the file type first (JPG only), then the KB range. If you chose Aadhaar-based authentication, SSC says the form will not be rejected for photo or signature quality, but you still need a valid signature upload.
