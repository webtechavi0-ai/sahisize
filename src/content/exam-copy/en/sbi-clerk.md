SBI Junior Associate is one of the few bank forms where the official notice is easy to read, and the upload rules are strict. You need four files: photo, signature, left thumb impression and a handwritten declaration. Plan for about twenty minutes and one sheet of white paper.

### Get all four files right in one sitting

1. **Write first, photograph later.** On one A4 sheet, sign once near the top, put your left thumb impression in the middle, and write the declaration at the bottom. One sheet, one photo session.
2. **Copy the SBI declaration, not an old IBPS one.** The SBI text has your Date of Birth after your name and ends with "The signature, photograph and left thumb impression is of mine." Write it in English, in running handwriting, with black ink. No capital letters.
3. **Take the photo against a light wall** in daylight, then crop it here to 200×230 px. Auto-fit keeps it between 20 and 50 KB.
4. **Crop each part of the sheet separately**: signature to 140×60 px (10–20 KB), thumb to 240×240 px (20–50 KB), declaration to 800×400 px (50–100 KB).
5. **Download the ZIP** and keep it. The live photo is taken separately in the form, by webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone.

### What is different about SBI

- The declaration text includes your **Date of Birth**. This is the most common reason people redo the declaration.
- The pixel sizes are written as "preferred", but the KB ranges are enforced. SahiSize uses the exact pixels anyway, so nothing is left to chance.
- SBI says a mismatch between your uploaded signature and the one on the call letter or attendance sheet can lead to disqualification.

### When to prepare

The 2026 notice came out in August, and SBI clerk forms usually open around **August–September**. Make your files before the window opens; the form logs you out if you take too long.

### If the portal still rejects a file

Check the Pass check card for the red row. A KB error means the file drifted out of range after editing; run Auto-fit again. "Not a valid image" usually means a HEIC or PNG file was renamed; download a fresh JPG from SahiSize instead.
