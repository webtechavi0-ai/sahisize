### What the NTPC form asks for

- **No photo upload.** A live photo is captured with your webcam or phone front camera.
- **Signature**: JPG, at least 140×60 px, **30–49 KB**, at least 100 DPI, black ink, centred in a 35×20 mm box, running handwriting.
- **SC/ST certificate**: PDF under 400 KB, only if you want a free train travel pass.

### Before you start the form

1. Sign in running handwriting with a black pen on plain white paper.
2. Make `sign.jpg` here. Auto-fit takes it into the 30–49 KB range without making it blurry.
3. If you need the travel pass, make `scst.pdf` from your latest valid certificate.
4. Set up the live photo spot: white wall, light in front of you, phone or webcam at eye level.
5. Wear plain dark clothes that cover shoulders and chest, so you stand out from the white background.

### The live photo, in detail

RRB asks that your head fills at least half the photo and nothing below the chest is visible. Keep a neutral face with eyes fully open; the notice says a photo with closed eyes will not be captured. Remove cap, mask and glasses, and keep hair off your face. You can preview and retake until you submit.

### Matching later

The live photo is checked against your photo ID. If they do not match, you will be asked to download Annexure VIII, get it attested and upload it. The signature is compared at the CBT, DV, medical and appointment.
