IBPS RRB Officer Scale I is recruitment for Regional Rural Banks (Gramin Banks), not the railways. The 2026 cycle (CRP RRBs-XV) took applications from **1 to 21 September 2026**. PO and Office Assistant posts share one notification, so one set of files works for both.

### Uploads

| Document | Pixels | KB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo | 200×230 | 20–50 |
| Signature | 140×60 | 10–20 |
| Left thumb | 240×240 | 20–50 |
| Declaration | 800×400 | 50–100 |
| 10th certificate (PDF, A4) | — | up to 500 |

The certificate limit is taken from the IBPS PO 2026 notice on the same portal; RRB guides say the same, but we have not yet read it in the RRB notice itself, so it is marked "to verify".

### One evening plan

- Gather your 10th certificate, a black pen, a stamp pad and two sheets of white paper.
- Sheet one: sign a few times, then press your left thumb once.
- Sheet two: write the IBPS declaration in English in running handwriting. Leave margins so the crop is easy.
- Take the photo last, in daylight, against a light wall.
- Process everything here and download the ZIP before you start the form.

### Things RRB applicants often get wrong

- Searching "RRB photo size" and getting the Railway RRB rules. Railway forms take a live photo and a 30–49 KB signature; Gramin Bank forms do not.
- Writing the declaration in Hindi or in capital letters. IBPS accepts only English in your own handwriting.
- Uploading a phone photo of the certificate as an image instead of a PDF.

### If a file is refused

Read the error, find the matching row in the Pass check card, and redo only that file. Do not re-compress a file you already downloaded; start from the original photo so it stays sharp.
