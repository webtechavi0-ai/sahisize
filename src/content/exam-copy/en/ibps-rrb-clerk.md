IBPS RRB Office Assistant (Multipurpose) is the clerk-level post in Regional Rural Banks. It is in the same CRP RRBs-XV notification as the officer post, and the 2026 window was **1–21 September 2026**.

### What you upload

Four images on the usual IBPS specs, plus the 10th certificate if your form shows that field:

- Photo 200×230 px, 20–50 KB.
- Signature 140×60 px, 10–20 KB.
- Left thumb impression 240×240 px, 20–50 KB.
- Handwritten declaration 800×400 px, 50–100 KB.
- Class 10 certificate as one PDF on A4, up to 500 KB (from the IBPS PO 2026 notice; not yet confirmed in the RRB notice).

### Applying from a cyber cafe or CSC

Many Office Assistant candidates apply through a cafe or Common Service Centre. You can save time and money by preparing the files yourself first:

1. Make all files here on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us; it runs on your device.
2. Download the ZIP and send it to yourself, or copy it to a pen drive.
3. At the cafe, the operator only needs to pick `photo.jpg`, `sign.jpg`, `thumb.jpg` and `decl.jpg` from the folder. The names make it hard to mix them up.
4. You still need to be there for the live photo, which is taken by webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone.

### The declaration, briefly

Use the IBPS text on this page. Write your name in the blank, keep the rest exactly as given, and write in normal running English handwriting with black ink. Capital letters are not accepted.

### Reusing last year's files

Sizes have not changed, so last year's signature and thumb usually still pass. Use a recent photo and write a fresh declaration.

### When the form complains

A KB error means the file is outside its range. Re-run Auto-fit on the original picture. If the preview looks blurry or smudged, retake the photo of the paper in better light; IBPS can reject unclear uploads even if the size is right.
