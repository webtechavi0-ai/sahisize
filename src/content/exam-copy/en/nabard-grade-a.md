NABARD Grade A (Assistant Manager) runs its application on the same IBPS-style portal used by most bank exams, so the uploads are the familiar four. The 2025 cycle opened in **November**, and that is when to expect the next one. Our values are checked against the official NABARD advertisement for the 2025 cycle (applications 8–30 November 2025).

### The uploads

- Photo, 200×230 px, 20–50 KB
- Signature, 140×60 px, 10–20 KB
- Left thumb impression, 240×240 px, 20–50 KB
- Handwritten declaration, 800×400 px, 50–100 KB

All are JPG at 200 DPI. There is also a live photo captured in the form by webcam or by scanning a QR code.

### If you also apply for RBI Grade B or IBPS SO

Officer-level candidates often apply to NABARD, RBI Grade B and IBPS SO in the same year. The photo, signature and thumb specs are the same, so keep one master set:

1. Make the files once with a recent photo.
2. Store the ZIP in a folder named by month.
3. For each new form, check the photo is still recent and the declaration text matches that notice.
4. Write a fresh declaration if the wording differs.

### Writing the declaration

We show the text printed in the NABARD advertisement; it is the same wording that IBPS and RBI use. Write in English, in your own running handwriting, with black ink on white paper. Capital letters are not accepted.

### Getting the thumb right

Press once on the ink pad and once, flat, on paper, with only a slight roll so the edges print and no sliding. Too much ink fills the ridges; too little leaves gaps. Clean thumb then makes the background pure white and keeps the impression sharp at 240×240 px.

### If the portal still rejects a file

Look at the exact message. A KB message means re-running Auto-fit on the original picture. A message about clarity means retaking the picture of the paper in daylight, flat and without shadows. Keep a screenshot of any error; it helps if you need to contact the helpdesk.
