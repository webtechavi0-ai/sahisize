NEET UG asks for more uploads than any other NTA exam: a photo, a signature, one sheet of finger and thumb prints from both hands, and several PDFs. NTA gives no pixel sizes, only KB ranges, and it does not let you correct the photo, signature or prints after you submit. So it pays to prepare everything before you open the form.

### Get every file right in one sitting

1. **Photo.** Take a new one in the exam year (the bulletin sets the earliest date), against a white wall, with your face filling about 80% of the frame and both ears showing. Colour or black and white both work. SahiSize keeps a 3.5×4.5 cm shape and lands it inside 10–200 KB.
2. **Signature.** Black pen on white paper. Crop close to the ink. The limit is 10–100 KB.
3. **Finger and thumb sheet.** Ink all four fingers and the thumb of each hand. Press the left hand on the left half of one white A4 sheet and the right hand on the right half. Photograph it in landscape.
4. **PDFs.** Class 10 certificate and address proof are needed by everyone. Category and PwD certificates only if they apply to you. Each one is a single PDF of 50–300 KB.

### Why 50–200 KB for the prints

The bulletin gives two ranges for the finger and thumb impressions: 10–200 KB in the list of uploads and 50–300 KB in the detailed instructions. A file between 50 and 200 KB meets both, so that is what SahiSize targets.

### Live photo

The form also captures a live photo and matches it with your Aadhaar record. Sit facing a window, remove a cap or mask, and keep the same look as your uploaded photo.

### Timing

For NEET UG 2026 the form ran from 08 February to 08 March 2026. Expect the next one around February–March.

### If the portal still rejects a file

Check the KB on the Pass check card first; most errors are size. If a PDF is under 50 KB, add the page again here so the quality goes up. If the prints look grey, retake with less ink and keep Clean thumb on.
