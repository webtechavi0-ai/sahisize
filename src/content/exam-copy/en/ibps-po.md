IBPS PO (CRP PO/MT-XVI) asks for five uploads, not four. Besides the usual photo, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration, the 2026 notice adds your **Class 10 (SSC/SSLC) certificate** as a PDF. The 2026 application window was 01–21 July 2026.

### The five files at a glance

- **Photo**: 200×230 px, 20–50 KB, JPG, light background.
- **Signature**: 140×60 px, 10–20 KB, black ink, not in capitals.
- **Left thumb**: 240×240 px, 20–50 KB, black or blue ink.
- **Declaration**: 800×400 px, 50–100 KB, English, your own handwriting.
- **10th certificate**: one PDF on A4 pages, not more than 500 KB.

### Step by step

1. Scan or photograph your 10th certificate first. If it has two pages, add both here; SahiSize joins them into one A4 PDF under 500 KB and keeps the text readable.
2. On a white sheet, sign, put the left thumb impression, and write the IBPS declaration: "I, ______ (Name of the candidate), hereby declare that all the information submitted by me in the application form is correct, true and valid. I will present the supporting documents as and when required." Unlike SBI, there is no Date of Birth line.
3. Take the photo against a light wall, then crop each item here and run Auto-fit.
4. Keep the ZIP. IBPS will not let you move to the next stage of the form until the photo and signature meet the specs.

### Special cases IBPS allows

- No left thumb: use the right thumb. No thumbs: a finger of the left hand, starting with the forefinger, and write which finger it is.
- Cannot write: get the declaration typed and put your left thumb impression below it.
- A declaration in Hindi or in someone else's handwriting is treated as invalid.

### If the portal still says no

Look at which upload failed. For the PDF, the usual problem is a phone scan saved as a huge image; rebuild it here. For images, run Auto-fit from the original photo again rather than compressing an already small file twice.
