RRB Assistant Loco Pilot (CEN 01/2026) uses the current RRB pattern: **no photo upload**, a live photo in the form, and a signature with an unusual minimum size. The ALP form usually opens around **February–March**.

### The signature rule that catches people

- JPG, **at least 140×60 px**.
- **30–49 KB**. Note the minimum: most exams fail you for being too big; RRB also fails you for being too small.
- Scanned at **100 DPI or more**.
- Black ink, white paper, centred in a **35×20 mm** box.
- Running handwriting, not block or capital letters.

A clean signature normally compresses to 10–20 KB, which is under the RRB minimum. SahiSize first makes the image bigger (step by step up to 600 px wide, usually about 600×257 px), then raises quality, and only if still needed adds invisible padding until it reaches 30–49 KB. The signature looks the same.

### Live photo: dress and background

RRB's live photo rules are stricter than SSC's:

- Pure plain white background, nothing behind you.
- Plain dark, non-white clothes covering shoulders and chest.
- Camera at eye level, neutral face, eyes fully open.
- Your head should fill at least half the photo; nothing below the chest.
- No cap, mask or glasses; hair must not cover the face.

The live photo is matched with your photo ID. If it does not match, the form asks for an attested Annexure.

### SC/ST free travel pass

If you are SC/ST and want a free train pass to the exam, upload your certificate as a PDF under 400 KB. SahiSize caps it at 399 KB to stay safely under.

### Rejection grounds RRB lists

Non-white background, non-black ink, block or capital letters, poor resolution, incomplete signature, a thumb impression in place of a signature, or someone else's signature. Each one can cancel the application at any stage, even after the exam.

### If the upload is refused

Most failures are under 30 KB. Run Auto-fit on the original picture, not on a compressed copy, and check the Pass check card shows 30–49 KB.
