CUET UG is the lightest NTA form for uploads. Most candidates need only two files: a photo and a signature. The catch is the signature limit, which is tighter than in NEET or JEE Main.

### The two files most people need

**Photo.** A recent colour passport-size photo on a white background. Your face, including both ears, should cover about 80% of the image, without a mask. The limit is 10–200 KB in JPG. NTA gives no pixel size, so SahiSize uses 3.5×4.5 cm proportions and only shrinks the image if it would go over 200 KB.

**Signature.** Black ink on white paper, 10–50 KB in JPG. If you saved a signature for NEET or JEE Main, check its size: those forms allow up to 100 KB, so an older file may be too big for CUET.

### Only if it applies to you

- A disability or UDID certificate, as one PDF of 50–300 KB.
- An ID proof as a 10–200 KB JPG, only if you verify your identity without Aadhaar or DigiLocker.

### Before you sit down to apply

1. Take the photo in daylight facing a window. Keep the camera at eye level.
2. Sign two or three times and pick the cleanest one.
3. Run both through the CUET UG preset and check the Pass check card.
4. Print a few copies of the same photo file. The bulletin asks for an additional photo, the same as the uploaded one, on the attendance sheet at the centre.

### Live photo

During the form you also capture a live photo by webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone. It is compared with your Aadhaar photo, so avoid new glasses or a cap.

### Timing

The CUET UG 2026 bulletin came out in January 2026. Expect the form around January–February.

### If the portal still rejects it

A signature that fails is almost always over 50 KB. Crop closer to the ink and fit again. A photo that fails is usually a HEIC file from an iPhone; SahiSize saves a real JPG.
