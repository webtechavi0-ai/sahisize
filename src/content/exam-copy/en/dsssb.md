DSSSB applications go through OARS, and the photo there is unlike almost any other exam: it is a **postcard-size, upper-body photo**, not a passport-size face. If you reuse a bank-exam photo, it will be too small in both pixels and KB.

### What DSSSB asks for

- **Photo:** 480×672 px at 96 DPI, 50–300 KB. Upper body visible, with both ears and both shoulders in frame, on a light background.
- **Signature:** up to 20 KB, black ink. Sources list a 110×140 px box but disagree on which way round it goes.
- **Thumb impressions:** left and right thumbs as **two separate files**, each up to 40 KB.

### Two judgement calls we made

Sources give the signature limit as 40 KB or 20 KB. A file of 20 KB or less is accepted under both, so SahiSize stays at or below 20 KB.

For the signature box, a signature is wider than it is tall, so we output 140 wide × 110 high. If the portal says the dimensions are wrong, it may want 110×140; use custom size to swap them. We will update this page once the official specification is confirmed.

### One sitting, four files

1. Stand a step further back than for a passport photo, against a plain light wall. Make sure your shoulders and ears are visible and hair does not cover the ears.
2. Crop to the tall postcard frame and Auto-fit to 480×672 px.
3. Sign on white paper in black ink. Crop close and fit under 20 KB.
4. Ink your left thumb and press once on white paper. Do the same with the right thumb beside it.
5. Crop each thumb separately. Files come out as thumb_l.jpg and thumb_r.jpg, so you do not upload the same thumb twice.

### Timing

DSSSB publishes notices for different posts through the year, so there is no fixed season. Keep these files ready.

### If the portal still rejects it

Most photo rejections are a face-only crop or a file under 50 KB. For thumbs, check you did not upload the left impression in the right-thumb box.
