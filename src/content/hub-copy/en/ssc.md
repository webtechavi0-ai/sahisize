## What differs between SSC exams

Very little, and that is good news. Every SSC form asks for **one upload, the signature**, and every recent notice gives the same box: about 6.0 cm wide and 2.0 cm high, JPG, 10–20 KB. Two details are worth knowing:

- **SSC GD** says the 6.0×2.0 cm signature is scanned "at a resolution of 300 DPI", so its pixel size is bigger (about 709×236) than CGL, CHSL, CPO and MTS (about 472×157 at 200 DPI). The KB limit does not change.
- **SSC CGL and CPO** notices print 4.0×2.0 cm in one annexure and 6.0×2.0 cm in the main instructions. Both are "about", and the portal checks the file type and KB, so we follow the main instruction.

## The SSC photo is captured live

**You do not upload a photo for any SSC exam.** The form takes a live photo through your webcam or the mySSC app, and pointing the camera at an old printed photo gets the form rejected. Sit facing a window, camera at eye level, no cap, mask or spectacles, and retake it as often as you need before you submit.

Forms submitted with Aadhaar-based authentication are not rejected for photo or signature quality, but you still upload a signature, so make it right anyway.
