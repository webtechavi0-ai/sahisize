## What differs between RRB exams

For uploads, nothing. NTPC, Group D and ALP notices all print the same signature rule and the same optional SC/ST certificate. What changes is the calendar: each CEN opens in a different season, so the form you are filling decides the dates, not the sizes.

- **One signature file works for all three**, as long as it is your normal running signature in black ink.
- **The SC/ST certificate** is needed only if you ask for a free train pass to the exam. It is one PDF under 400 KB; SahiSize caps it at 399 KB to stay safely under.
- **Qualification does not change uploads.** A 10th-pass Group D applicant and a graduate NTPC applicant upload the same files.

## Why the 30 KB minimum matters

Most exams fail a file for being too big. RRB also fails it for being too small. A clean signature at 140×60 pixels compresses to well under 30 KB, so a quick resize leaves you below the range. SahiSize first enlarges the image step by step, up to 600 px wide (usually about 600×257 px), then raises the quality, and pads the file invisibly only as a last resort. The signature looks the same; the file passes.

## The RRB photo is captured live

**You do not upload a photo for RRB.** The live photo is stricter than SSC's, and it is compared with your photo ID:

- A pure plain white background, nothing behind you.
- Plain dark, non-white clothes that cover your shoulders and chest.
- Camera at eye level, neutral face, eyes fully open, head filling at least half the frame.
- No cap, mask or glasses, and no strong light behind you.

If the live photo does not match your ID, the form asks for an attested annexure, which costs you days. Five minutes of setting up the light saves that.

## Your signature follows you

RRB compares the uploaded signature at the CBT, document verification, the medical and joining. Use the signature you will sign with for years, not a special neat version, and keep the paper you signed on.
