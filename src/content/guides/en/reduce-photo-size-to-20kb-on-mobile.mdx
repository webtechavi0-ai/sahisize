"Photo 20 kb" is one of the most searched phrases before every exam season. A phone camera photo is 2–5 MB. The form wants something like 20 KB. That is 100 to 250 times smaller — and most people do it from their phone, not a computer.

This guide shows how to get a photo or signature to 20 KB on mobile, without the face turning into a blurry mess. (If you have been searching "photo ka size kam kaise kare", this is the answer.)

## First: is it "under 20 KB" or "20 to 50 KB"?

This is the trap. Read your notice carefully:

- **IBPS / SBI signature:** 10–20 KB. So 20 KB is the **maximum**.
- **IBPS / SBI photo:** 20–50 KB. So 20 KB is the **minimum** — a 15 KB photo gets rejected as too small.
- **SSC signature:** 10–20 KB.
- **Bihar Police photo and signature:** 15–25 KB.

So "make it 20 KB" can mean very different things. Always check the latest official notice, then pick a target inside the range — not exactly on the edge.

## Why most apps make it blurry

Most "compress" apps only lower JPG quality. A 3000 × 4000 px photo at very low quality might reach 20 KB, but it looks blocky and smeared.

The better method:

1. **Crop first.** Throw away background you do not need.
2. **Reduce pixels.** A passport photo does not need 12 million pixels. 200 × 230 or 276 × 354 px is plenty.
3. **Then adjust quality.** With fewer pixels, the photo hits 20 KB at a good quality.

SahiSize does this order automatically. More on why pixels and KB are different in [KB vs pixels — simple explanation](/guides/photo-size-kb-vs-pixels).

## Tips for a sharp 20 KB file

- **Start from the original.** Do not use a photo you received on WhatsApp — it is already compressed and loses more detail each time.
- **Plain background.** A plain light wall compresses far better than a patterned curtain. Detail costs KB.
- **Good light.** Dark, grainy photos have noise, and noise needs more KB. Daylight from a window is best.
- **Signatures compress easily.** A clean black-on-white signature usually reaches 10–20 KB at high quality. Use [Signature Resize](/tools/signature-resize), which also whitens the paper.
- **Aim for the middle.** For a 10–20 KB range, about 15 KB is safest. Some portals count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes, so a 19.9 KB file can fail.

If your target is a range like 20–50 KB, going too low is also an error. Signatures and plain photos often come out **too small** — 12 KB, 15 KB — even at full quality.

## On iPhone

iPhone photos are often saved as **HEIC**, which many portals do not accept. SahiSize reads HEIC and outputs a normal JPG, so you can compress and convert in one step. In Safari, the downloaded file goes to the Files app → Downloads.

## On Android

Chrome saves downloads to the Downloads folder. When the exam portal asks you to choose a file, tap **Browse** or **Files**, then **Downloads**. Pick the file named like `photo.jpg`.

## FAQ

### How do I check the size of a photo on my phone?

On Android, open the photo in Files and tap the info (i) icon. On iPhone, open it in the Files app and long-press → Get Info. SahiSize also shows the KB before you download.

### My photo is 20 KB on my phone, but the portal says "image size too large". Why?

Your phone may round. The file might be 20.4 KB, or the portal may count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes. Aim for 17–18 KB when the maximum is 20 KB.

### Can I reduce photo size to 20 KB without losing quality?

Some loss is unavoidable at 20 KB, but if you crop first and reduce pixels to the size the exam wants, the photo still looks clear at normal viewing size.

### Will compressing change my face or remove the background?

No. SahiSize only crops, resizes and compresses. It does not edit your face or change the background.
