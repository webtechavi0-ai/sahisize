The signature is the one document every exam asks for. SSC and RRB no longer let you upload a photo — it is captured live — but you still upload a signature. And a bad signature scan is one of the top reasons for rejection.

You do not need a scanner. A phone camera is enough if you do five things right.

## What you need

- A **plain white A4 sheet** (no lines, no stains). Printer paper is perfect.
- A **black gel pen** or a black ball pen that writes dark. Most notices say black ink; banking accepts black, RRB asks for black.
- Daylight from a window, or a bright room light.
- Your phone.

Avoid blue ballpoint if you can. It turns pale grey after compression and some portals reject faint signatures.

## Step 1: Sign properly

1. Sign in your **normal running signature** — the one you will also sign at the exam centre. If it differs on the day, you can be stopped.
2. **Do not sign in capital letters.** IBPS, SBI and RBI notices say this clearly. A signature in block capitals is treated as invalid.
3. Sign **bigger than usual** — about 6–8 cm wide. A bigger signature keeps its detail when it is shrunk to 140 × 60 pixels.
4. Sign two or three times on the sheet. Pick the cleanest one later.

### Special formats

- **UPSC (CSE, NDA, CDS):** sign **three times, one below another**, and capture all three in one image. The final image is square, 350–500 px, 20–100 KB.
- **Bihar Police Constable:** two separate signature files — one in **Hindi**, one in **English**, each 15–25 KB.
- **RRB:** the signature should sit centred in a 35 × 20 mm box. Sign so it fits in a box of that shape.

## Step 2: Light it right

The goal is pure white paper and pure black ink, with no shadow.

- Put the sheet on a flat table near a window.
- Hold the phone **directly above** the paper, parallel to it. A tilted phone makes the signature look slanted.
- Check that your hand or phone is not casting a shadow on the signature. Move to the side of the light if needed.
- Turn the flash **off**. Flash makes a shiny spot in the middle.

## Step 3: Take the photo

- Fill most of the screen with the signature, leaving a small margin.
- Tap on the signature to focus. Wait a second before you click.
- Take two or three photos. Zoom in on the phone and pick the sharpest.

Do not use the phone's document-scanner mode with heavy filters. It sometimes eats the thin parts of the signature.

## Signature sizes for common exams

Always check the latest official notice — these are the values from the 2025–26 notices.

| Exam | Pixels | KB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IBPS PO](/exam/ibps-po), SBI, RBI | 140 × 60 px | 10–20 KB |
| [SSC CGL](/exam/ssc-cgl), CHSL, MTS, CPO | 6.0 × 2.0 cm ≈ 472 × 157 px at 200 DPI | 10–20 KB |
| [RRB ALP](/exam/rrb-alp), Group D, NTPC | at least 140 × 60 px | 30–49 KB |
| NEET UG, JEE Main | KB only | 10–100 KB |
| CUET UG, UGC NET | KB only | 10–50 KB |
| [UPSC CSE](/exam/upsc-cse) | 350–500 px square (3 signatures) | 20–100 KB |
| CTET | 3.5 × 1.5 cm | 3–30 KB |

SSC gives only centimetres. At 200 DPI, 6.0 × 2.0 cm works out to about 472 × 157 pixels — that is what SahiSize uses by default.

## The RRB problem: signature too small in KB

RRB wants 30–49 KB. A clean black-on-white signature is so simple that it often compresses to only 10–15 KB, even at full quality. Then the portal says the file is too small.

## Common mistakes

- **Signature on lined or ruled paper.** The lines show up. Use plain paper.
- **Photo of the screen.** Never photograph a signature shown on another screen; it becomes blurry with moiré lines.
- **Using an old signature image.** If you signed differently three years ago, make a fresh one.
- **Wrong orientation.** The image must be landscape (wider than tall) for most exams, except UPSC which is square.
- **Saving from WhatsApp.** WhatsApp re-compresses images. Transfer by cable or use the original file.

## FAQ

### Can I sign on paper with blue ink?

Some exams allow blue, but black is safer: it stays dark after compression. RRB and SSC prefer black. Check your notice.

### Can I use a digital signature drawn on the screen?

No. Exams want your handwritten signature on paper, photographed or scanned. A finger-drawn signature looks different from how you sign at the centre.

### My signature is very small. Will it still work?

Crop tightly so the ink fills the box. If it looks thin and broken after resizing, sign again bigger with a thicker pen.
