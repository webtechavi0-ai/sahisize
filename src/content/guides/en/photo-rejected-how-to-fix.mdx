You filled the whole form, clicked **Upload**, and the portal said "Image size too large", "Invalid file" or "Photo does not meet the specification". Or worse — the upload went through, and weeks later your application was rejected at the scrutiny stage.

Almost every photo upload error comes from one of eight problems. Each one has a simple fix. This guide goes through them one by one, with the actual numbers that exam portals check.

## First, find out what the portal actually wants

Every exam has its own rules. A photo that works for NEET can fail on the IBPS portal. Before you change anything, note these four things from the notice:

1. **File size range in KB** — for example 20–50 KB.
2. **Pixel size** — for example 200 × 230 px (width × height). Some exams give only KB, no pixels.
3. **Format** — almost always JPG/JPEG for photos and signatures.
4. **Content rules** — background colour, face size, ink colour, name and date on the photo, and so on.

Here are a few real examples (always check the latest official notice — numbers change between cycles):

| Exam | Photo | Signature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IBPS PO](/exam/ibps-po), [SBI Clerk](/exam/sbi-clerk) | 200 × 230 px, 20–50 KB | 140 × 60 px, 10–20 KB |
| [NEET UG](/exam/neet-ug) | 10–200 KB, white background | 10–100 KB |

## Reason 1: File is too big (above max KB)

This is the most common error. A phone camera photo is usually 2–5 MB, which is 2,000–5,000 KB. The IBPS limit is 50 KB. That is 50 to 100 times smaller.

**Fix:** Do not just lower the "quality" slider in a random app and hope. Use [Compress Image](/tools/compress-image) and set the exact target. If you need a common size, there are ready pages: [compress to 20 KB](/tools/compress-image-to-20kb), [compress to 50 KB](/tools/compress-image-to-50kb), [compress to 100 KB](/tools/compress-image-to-100kb). SahiSize finds the highest quality that still fits under the limit, so your face stays sharp.

## Reason 2: File is too small (below min KB)

Many people do not know this one exists. IBPS wants the photo to be **at least** 20 KB. RRB wants the signature to be **at least 30 KB**. If you over-compress, the portal rejects it as too small.

A plain signature on white paper compresses very well, so it often comes out at 8–15 KB — too small for RRB's 30–49 KB range.

**Fix:** Use a tool that targets a *range*, not just a maximum. SahiSize's [Signature Resize](/tools/signature-resize) and the exam pages check both ends: the file must be above the minimum, and it lands just under the maximum with a small safety margin. For RRB, it adds a tiny amount of invisible detail or padding so the file reaches 30 KB without changing how the signature looks.

## Reason 3: Wrong pixel size

"Photo dimensions should be 200 × 230 pixels." If you upload 1080 × 1440, the portal may reject it or squash it into a stretched face.

**Fix:** Crop to the right shape first, then resize. The shape (aspect ratio) matters. 200 × 230 is a little taller than wide; a phone selfie is much taller. Your exam page and the [Custom size](/tools/custom-preset) tool show a crop box locked to the exact shape. [Resize Image](/tools/resize-image) only changes the pixel count, so crop first or the picture gets squashed. Read [KB vs pixels](/guides/photo-size-kb-vs-pixels) if this part is confusing.

## Reason 4: Wrong format

The portal says JPG and you uploaded PNG, HEIC (iPhone) or WEBP (WhatsApp sometimes). Some portals also reject `.jpeg` files with odd internal settings, like progressive JPGs or CMYK colour.

**Fix:** Convert with [Convert Image](/tools/convert-image). SahiSize always outputs a plain baseline JPG in normal sRGB colour, which every portal we know of accepts. More on formats in [JPG vs PNG vs PDF](/guides/jpg-vs-png-vs-pdf).

## Reason 5: Background is not plain or light

Most notices say "light-coloured, preferably white, background". NTA exams (NEET, JEE, CUET) say white. A photo taken in front of a curtain, cupboard or patterned wall can be rejected at scrutiny even if the upload worked.

**Fix:** Retake the photo standing in front of a plain light wall, a metre away from it so your shadow does not fall on it. Light should come from the front — face a window in daytime.

## Reason 6: Face too small, too large or covered

UPSC wants the face to cover about 75% of the photo, with both ears visible. NEET asks for about 80% face including ears. Caps, dark glasses and hair covering the eyes are not allowed. Religious headwear is fine as long as the face is fully visible.

**Fix:** Use the crop box. Keep the top of your head close to the top edge and your chin around three-quarters of the way down. Remove glasses if they reflect light.

## Reason 7: Blurred, dark or tiny signature

SSC rejects signatures that are blurred or too small. A signature written with a thin blue ballpoint, photographed from far away, becomes a faint grey line after compression.

**Fix:** Sign with a black gel pen, sign big, and crop tight so the ink fills most of the box. Our [signature scanning guide](/guides/scan-signature-with-phone) covers this step by step. Also: banking exams say the signature must **not be in capital letters**.

## Reason 8: Wrong photo, old photo or missing details

- The photo must be recent. NEET has asked for a photo taken on or after 1 January of the exam year; Bihar Police asked for one not older than 2 months.
- Some state exams (MPPSC, HSSC) want your **name and date written on the photo**.
- UPSC wants **three signatures** one below another in one image; Bihar Police wants **two signatures**, one in Hindi and one in English.
- Banking and NTA exams also take a live photo at the exam centre or in the form — your uploaded photo must match your face on the day.

**Fix:** Read the content rules on your exam page before you click the photo, not after.

## FAQ

### The portal says "image size too large" but my file is 49 KB. Why?

Some portals count 1 KB as 1,000 bytes, others as 1,024. A 49 KB file is 50,176 bytes — over a 50 KB limit if the portal counts in thousands. SahiSize aims just under the top of the limit with a safety margin, so the file passes whichever way the portal counts.

### My upload worked. Can it still be rejected later?

Yes. The portal only checks size and format. A human or software checks background, face and signature quality later. Follow the content rules too.

### Can I use the same photo for every exam?

Only if the specs match. IBPS, SBI and RBI share the same 200 × 230 px, 20–50 KB spec, so one file works for all of them. NEET or UPSC need a different file.
